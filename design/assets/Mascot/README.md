# Mascot

The Pun sprinter: a grape elephant in yellow high-tops, mid-dash, winking (the wink is the pun), with a lightning-bolt cowlick. Transparent PNGs with the outline baked in.

- `pun-sprinter.png`: full size (about 1240px). Hero panels, social cards, print.
- `pun-sprinter-512.png`: 512px. Docs, 404 page, avatars, anything under 300px wide.

Rules: place it on `lilac`, `zap`, `paper` or `card`; on the dark theme it carries its own light edge. Always facing right (the direction of travel), never mirrored, rotated or recoloured, never cropped through the sneakers or the trunk. Give it room: at least 12% of its width clear on every side inside a panel. Below 64px use `pun-bolt.svg` from Logos instead. A vector redraw is still to come for favicons and stickers.
