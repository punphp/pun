# Burst

A round sticker badge rotated `tilt-burst` (10deg) for one number: the multiple, a timing, "=" for parity. Pinned to the corner of a panel or card, half off its edge.

- Props: `children` (2 to 4 characters), `sub` (one tiny mono word), `tone` (`gum` default, `zap`, `mint`, `lilac`).
- Max two per screen. The number must come from a real measurement.
