Built for Laravel. Not bolted on.

Pun knows your app the way Larastan and rector-laravel do, without installing either. Nothing to turn on: it reads your composer.lock.

pun.tomlTOML
# Laravel and Pest: found in composer.lock
[format]
preset = "laravel"

  • Larastan, built in

    Models, relations, facades, macros, container bindings and config, understood natively by pun analyse.

  • rector-laravel rules

    Laravel upgrades and code-quality rewrites in pun lint, applied only when you say so.

  • Pint's laravel preset

    The default for pun format. Same bytes Pint writes on every Laravel project.

  • Tests that read well

    assertStatus(404) becomes assertNotFound(), $this->faker becomes fake(), and Pest expectations get specific.

  • App rules

    Flags $request->all(), $casts properties, named migrations and middleware set in controllers.

  • Blade, too

    pun format --blade formats your Blade templates in the same run as your PHP.
